### Defending

- Pressing (Build Pressure on the Ball)
    - Recognize pressing triggers and take advantage
        - Poor touches/passes
        - Isolated opponents
    - Apply pressure in a coordinated manner
    - Limit time + space on the ball
- Compactness (Create Defending Shape)
    - Reduce space
    - Limit gaps and exposure to quick passes and dribbles
    - Deny the Finish
    - Recover when Pressure is Broken
- Organization
    - On and off ball communication and organization
    - Strong leadership
    - Vocalized positioning adjustments
    - Individual accountability
    - Anticipate to Counter

### Attacking

- Positional Play
    - Specified zones to operate in (Create Attacking Shape)
    - Diamonds + Triangles to create options and support
        - Provide Options to Play Forward
    - Anticipate Opponent Counter
- Possession with Purpose
    - Build toward scoring opportunities
    - Look to break lines and drive forward (Break Lines to Advance Attack)
- Attacking
    - Actions to create scoring opportunities
    - Speed of play
    - Positional interchanges (rotations, overlaps, etc)
    - Finish the Attack

### Reclaiming Possession (ATK > DEF)

- Counter Pressing
    - Pursuit of ball immediately (Apply Immediate Pressure)
    - Cut off passing options and outlets
        - Take Countermeasures
    - Prevent forward movement
    - Delay the Counter Attack
- Mentality to Regain Possession
    - Recover with Speed
    - Deny Finish from Counter Attack
- Anticipate the Counter

### Attacking Transition (DEF > ATK)

- Create a Goal Scoring Opportunity
    - Decisive and Purposeful actions
        - Play Forward Quickly
        - Join the Attack with Speed
    - Identify and Exploit gaps
        - Catch opponents off balance
        - Finish Counter Attack as Fast as Possible
    - Verticality with a strategy
        - Not just running straight ahead
        - Finding opportunities to break lines
- Creation vs Possession
    - Control the ball while advancing (Secure the Ball)
    - Balance vertical play and possession-based buildup
    - Don't sacrifice control for speed
- Mentality to Exploit the Opponent
    - Proactive thinking
    - Looking for ways to unbalance opposition
    - Urgency and Intention
