### Overview

We need to revise the logo.

The current design uses a single larger 'SIDE' word next to half sized 'LINE' and 'KICK' words stacked on top of each other. The word 'SIDE' has a horizontal gap in it that matches the size of the gap between 'LINE' and 'KICK'

The goal with this design was to communicate the name 'SIDELINE SIDEKICK' but it is instead being interpreted as 'SIDE LINE KICK'.

To address this, you need to modify the logo in the following ways:

- We will adjust the logo to be a 2 color design, one color being the primary logo color and one color being a shadowed color.
- We will create a logo that has 'SIDELINE' wording across the top line, and an angled 'SIDEKICK' wording in a shadowed appearance below it.
- To do this, we will revert to the version where the word 'SIDE' has no horizontal gap.
- We will shrink the word 'SIDE' vertically to match the same height of the word 'LINE'
- We will replace the bottom design with the shadowed wording that says 'SIDEKICK'
- Make 3 versions, one where the bottom shadowed wording appears to be at different skews. The idea would be that the sun (not shown in the logo) is up at the top left of the logo, causing the shadow. Imagine the sun is there to the left sometime between 8 and 10 in the morning.
